This browser demonstration illustrates microlensing with a few editable stars. It is not the full Microcaustics research simulator. Calculations run on your device and require a compatible GPU-enabled browser.

The disk uses a Novikov–Thorne temperature profile, with adjustable temperature slope and approximate gravitational and Doppler shifts. At fixed Eddington ratio, spin changes the accretion rate and emitting size. Black-hole light bending and intrinsic variability are omitted. Colored contours enclose half the light in each band. The black ellipse marks the projected ISCO. Dashed markers indicate detected disk-center caustic crossings, not every brightening event.

Example scale for a 1 M⊙ lens: source-plane RE = 5 × 1016 cm, zs = 1.695 and DA = 1.75 Gpc. Time is measured in tE = θE(1 M⊙) / ωref, for a fixed reference angular speed. Smooth convergence is 0.20 and shear is 0.25.
